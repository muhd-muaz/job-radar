How scoring works

Every job gets up to 100 points. Open any job to see its own sum.

Job title, up to 45
Data engineering, ETL, warehouse and pipeline titles get the full 45; data analyst, BA and BI titles get 30.
Skills, up to 55
The share of skills the ad asks for that I have. Skills I'm learning count as half a gap. An ad naming only one or two skills counts for less.
Points added
Up to +10 when the ad welcomes fresh graduates or juniors, and +8 for KL or Selangor.
Points off
15 to 40 for senior titles or 5+ years asked; 20 to 40 for contract or part-time; 25 when another language is required; 10 for elsewhere in Malaysia.
Caps
Contract and internship roles stop at 45. Titles outside my tracks (finance, sales and so on) stop at 50.
Bands
60 or more is a strong fit. 40 to 59 is worth a look. Under 40 is left off the radar and list.

Resume and who built this

Try it with your own CV

Anyone can use this, for any profession. Your CV stays in your own browser and is never uploaded.

  1. Get the template. Open the resume tool and press Template, or start from the sample person.
  2. Fill in your details and press Load my CV.
  3. Paste any job ad, or open a job here and press Tailored resume. Your matching lines move to the top, and you'll see what's missing and how to prepare.
  4. Save as PDF. It fits one A4 page and job-site scanners can read it.

Want your own daily job list for another profession? Copy the project on GitHub and change the keywords.
