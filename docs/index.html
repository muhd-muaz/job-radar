Try it with your own CV

Anyone can use this, for any profession. Your CV stays in your own browser and is never uploaded.

  1. Get the template. Open the resume tool and press Template, or start from the sample person.
  2. Fill in your details and press Load my CV.
  3. Paste any job ad, or open a job here and press Tailored resume. Your matching lines move to the top, and you'll see what's missing and how to prepare.
  4. Save as PDF. It fits one A4 page and job-site scanners can read it.
